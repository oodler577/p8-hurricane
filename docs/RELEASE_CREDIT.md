**Based on _Skipper Jim's Hurricane Tracking Chart_ by James R. Brown.**

Brown's original Commodore 64 program established the storm-recording and map-playback experience that p8-hurricane recreates and extends. We honor his work and preserve its name and author in every platform version. See [CREDITS.md](https://github.com/oodler577/p8-hurricane/blob/master/CREDITS.md) for the program's provenance.
